Privacy Policy

Choozy

Last updated: November 17, 2026

Choozy helps users explore food products, review nutritional information, scan barcodes, and compare prices at nearby stores.

1. Information We Collect

Depending on how you use Choozy, we may process:

  • Email addresses and account information used for registration and authentication.
  • Product information, feedback, and photos submitted by users.
  • Product scanning history and preferences associated with the app.
  • Location information when users request nearby store and price comparisons.

2. Camera Access

Choozy uses the device camera to scan product barcodes and capture product photos. Photos submitted to the app may be stored in our Supabase infrastructure.

3. Location Information

Location access is used to find nearby stores and display product prices within a selected radius of 1, 3, 5, or 10 km.

Choozy does not store users' geographic coordinates.

4. Data Storage

Choozy uses Supabase for database, authentication, and file storage services. Information is processed and stored to provide the application's features.

5. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and maintain app functionality.
  • Authenticate user accounts.
  • Display product information and history.
  • Process user-submitted product content.
  • Find nearby stores and compare prices.
  • Improve reliability and user experience.

6. Third-Party Services

Choozy uses Supabase to support application functionality. Information processed by service providers is subject to applicable privacy and security practices.

7. Data Retention and Deletion

Users may request deletion of their account and associated personal data by contacting us at the email address below. Requests will be handled in accordance with applicable legal requirements.

8. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect personal information. However, no method of electronic storage or transmission is completely secure.

9. Children's Privacy

Choozy is not specifically directed toward children. If we become aware of personal information collected from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take appropriate action.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy. Changes will be published on this page with an updated revision date.
