Choozy – Account Deletion

Last updated: November 19, 2026

Choozy respects your privacy and provides a way to request deletion of your account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Open your email application.
  2. Send an email to vetkot7@gmail.com .
  3. Use the subject: Choozy Account Deletion.
  4. Send the request from the email address associated with your Choozy account.

We may request additional verification to protect your account from unauthorized deletion requests.

Data covered by deletion

Once your request has been verified, we will delete your Choozy account, authentication information and personal data associated with your account, subject to applicable legal obligations.

This may include account-linked product submissions, uploaded images, preferences, and other personal records. Non-personal product catalogue and pricing information may be retained.

Data retention

Certain information may be retained where required by law, for security, or for legitimate record-keeping purposes. Backup copies may remain temporarily until their normal expiration.

Contact

For questions about account deletion, contact: vetkot7@gmail.com .
